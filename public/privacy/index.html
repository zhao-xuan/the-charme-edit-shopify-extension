Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026. Charmé Product Studio is operated under the name The Charmé Edit (“we”, “us”). Questions or privacy requests: support@thecharmeedit.com.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers merchants who install our Shopify app and shoppers who open a customizer from a merchant’s store. For shopper design data, the merchant determines why it is collected and we process it to provide the service. The merchant’s own privacy policy also applies to its store, orders and customer relationship.

Information we process

  • Merchant store domain, app installation credentials, product variant information, template settings and published customizer configuration.
  • Shopper-created designs, uploaded artwork after browser-side resizing, text, production notes, selected materials, and a private design link credential. Shoppers should avoid including sensitive personal information.
  • Anonymous customizer activity: a random session identifier in session storage, device category, selected template, design and cart handoff events, and approximate elapsed time. We do not use this report to identify shoppers.
  • Technical request information needed to secure and operate the service. A short-lived hash of IP address is used for rate limiting; it is not stored as a plain IP address in the design database.

How we use it and legal basis

We use merchant installation and product data to connect the app, show products and calculate a Shopify price; designs to save drafts, create production requests and enable merchant review; and anonymous activity to show the merchant how the customizer is used. For merchants, this is necessary to provide the contracted app service. For shopper designs, the merchant’s lawful basis applies and we act on the merchant’s instructions. We use limited technical information to protect the service and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and international processing

Shopify provides installation, product, theme and checkout services. Cloudflare hosts our app, database and delivery infrastructure. Merchant staff can access production requests for their own store. A shopper can access a design through its secret link, so keep that link private. We do not sell shopper designs or analytics. Shopify, Cloudflare and merchants may process data in other countries under their own applicable transfer safeguards.

Payments, cookies and storage

Checkout and payment happen in Shopify, not in this app. We do not collect payment card data. The Studio uses browser local storage to recover an unsaved design and a random in-memory identifier for anonymous activity during each page visit. Clearing browser data removes local design copies; server-saved designs remain until deleted separately.

Retention and deletion

We keep templates, customizers, designs and anonymous analytics while the merchant uses the app and as needed to handle outstanding production requests. Installation credentials are revoked when Shopify reports uninstall. On Shopify’s shop-redaction request we delete the shop’s stored credentials, templates, customizers, designs and analytics. Merchants can request earlier deletion of a design by providing its design ID. Our rate-limit hashes expire shortly after use. Shopify retains orders and payments under its and the merchant’s policies.

Your choices and rights

Merchants can unpublish a customizer and remove its theme App Block. Shoppers with a private design link can delete an unsubmitted draft in the Studio. For a submitted production request, ask the merchant to handle access, correction or deletion; include the design ID or private link so it can be located. Merchants can delete a design in the app after checking outstanding orders. You may also contact us at the address above. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy law. Because we do not link anonymous designs to Shopify customer IDs, an ID or private link may be needed to locate a specific design.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or law changes. We will update the effective date and notify merchants of material changes through the app or another appropriate channel.
